### thedotmack/claude-mem

**What it is:** Persistent Context Across Sessions for Every Agent – Captures everything your agent does during sessions, compresses it with AI, and injects relevant context back into future sessions. Works with Claude Code, OpenClaw, Codex, Gemini, Hermes, Copilot, OpenCode + More.

**Source:** https://github.com/thedotmack/claude-mem  ·  [Provenance: spotted on ManuAGI — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LRy1HQ_xnLc]

**What's good about it:** This repository provides a sophisticated memory system for AI agents that addresses the fundamental limitation of limited context retention. It automatically captures agent sessions, uses AI to compress the information, and intelligently injects relevant context back into future sessions. This enables agents to maintain long-term memory of interactions, preferences, and learned behaviors.

**Pros:**
- Specifically mentions Hermes in its compatibility list, indicating direct relevance to our system
- Uses AI-powered compression to make context injection intelligent rather than just storing raw data
- Actively maintained with recent updates (Oct 9, 2026) and pushed Oct 3, 2026
- Extremely popular with 98,797 stars and 8,657 forks indicating strong community validation
- Works with multiple agent systems including Claude Code, OpenClaw, Codex, Gemini, Hermes, Copilot, OpenCode + More
- Apache 2.0 licensed for permissive use

**Cons:**
- Requires understanding of how to integrate with our specific agent framework
- May introduce some overhead due to the AI compression/decompression process
- Need to evaluate potential conflicts with our existing memory consolidation systems
- As a third-party integration, adds dependency on external maintenance
- May require configuration to work optimally with our specific use cases

**Should we...**
- ✅ **Adopt it** — integrate into our stack (strongly recommended)

**Impact on our stack:** High — Would significantly enhance Hermes Agent and agentic-os by providing AI-powered persistent context across agent sessions, enabling agents to maintain long-term memory and learn from past interactions.

**Why:** Claude-mem directly addresses a critical limitation in our current agent systems: the inability to retain and leverage context across sessions. While we have basic memory functionality in our agentic-os (memory dashboard and consolidation job), claude-mem offers a sophisticated, AI-powered solution that automatically captures, compresses, and injects relevant context. This would enable our Hermes agents to maintain long-term memory of interactions, preferences, and learned behaviors, significantly improving their effectiveness over time. The fact that it specifically mentions Hermes in its compatibility list makes it particularly valuable for our stack. Given our focus on building reliable, intelligent AI agents for healthcare operations, this persistent context system would be a major enhancement to our agent capabilities.
