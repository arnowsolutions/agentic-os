# Sub-I Exit Interview Workflow — Dr. Schoenberg

## Overview
When a Sub-I / visiting medical student rotates through the department, an **Exit Interview with Dr. Schoenberg** must be scheduled during the **last week of their rotation**. This is added to the onboarding checklist.

## Process

### 1. At Onboarding (start of rotation)
- Note the student's **rotation end date**
- Log this as a pending task: "Schedule Exit Interview with Dr. Schoenberg for [Student Name]"
- **Request presentation documents** — students typically present on the **last Friday of their rotation**. Ask for their slides/deck at least 1 week before. Note the presentation date and add to calendar.

### 2. Request Presentation Materials
- **Last Friday of rotation = Sub-I presentation day**
- At the start of the rotation (or at minimum 2 weeks before), email the student requesting their presentation slides/deck
- Give them a deadline: slides due **1 week before their presentation date**
- Follow up if not received by the deadline
- Store presentation files in `/workspace/agentic-os/data/subi-presentations/`

### 2. Email Dr. Schoenberg for Availability
- Send an email to **mschoenb@montefiore.org**
- Request a **block of time during the last week** of the student's rotation
- Be specific: "I need to schedule [N] Exit Interview meetings during the week of [date]. What day/time block works for you?"
- CC yourself (sfrasier@montefiore.org)

### 3. Schedule the Meetings
Once Dr. Schoenberg provides his availability:
- Each meeting = **10 minutes**
- **5 minutes gap** between meetings
- **Back-to-back** during his selected block
- Via **Zoom** (use the standard department Zoom link or ask him for preference)
- Attach the **student's CV** to each meeting invitation

### 4. Send Calendar Invites
- Create events on the **urologyresidencyprogram@gmail.com** Google Calendar
- Include Dr. Schoenberg and the student as attendees
- Attach the student's CV to the invite
- Subject format: `"Sub-I Exit Interview — [Student Name]"`
- Duration: 10 min with 5 min gaps between

## CVs
- Student CVs are typically collected during credentialing/onboarding
- If you don't have the CV, ask the student to send one

## Email Template (for requesting availability)

```
Subject: Sub-I Exit Interview Availability — [Student Name]

Hi Dr. Schoenberg,

[Student Name] is rotating with us through [date range]. I'd like to schedule their Exit Interview during the last week of their rotation (week of [date]).

Do you have a block of time that week when you could do back-to-back 10-minute Zoom meetings? Please let me know your availability.

Thanks,
Shareef
```
